## Stage 1 design and tasks

- Add focused sandbox components and pure rules under `src/gameplay/sandbox/`; switch between CLASSIC and the new playground only during free play. Challenge starts remount classic mode.
- Domino: three adjacent manually popped bubbles in a single sweep trigger a finite delayed neighbour cascade, at most eight automatic pops. Only manual pops award action XP/Gems. Automatic board clears are visual events, and the board auto-flips.
- Catch practice: visible coloured nose and mint target arc; after at least one full turn, grab a coasting spinner in the arc. A perfect/near/missed catch changes feedback and Pip, without extra points. Existing spin awards remain unchanged.
- Orbit: gather up to eight crystals into a visible orbit and release them into a vortex. Each crystal can award its genuine capture once per wave; cancellation cannot repay it. Bank all eight to refresh. No passive vortex rewards.
- Pause/cancel on hidden/blur, bound timers/objects, respect reduced motion, keep all controls within the 320/390/430 portrait layouts.
- Write pure rule tests, play production pointer/touch gestures and verify classic score traces, saves, cancellation and repeated resets. Independent review before source publication.
